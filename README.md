# Analysis-of-Automative-Sales-Performance

## Project Overview
---
This project analyses an automotive and transport industry dataset to understand market performance and identify strategic opportunities to:
•	Optimize marketing campaigns 
•	Expand target audience reach 
•	Understand customer behaviour and segmentation 
•	Increase sales 
•	Identify new business opportunities

### Tool
MYSQL (SELECT, UPDATE, & CASE STATEMENTS, CTEs, aggregate & window functions)

### Data Source: 
Kaggle [Download Here](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kyanyoga/sample-sales-data)

### Analysis Process
---
1.	I Evaluated key business metrics, including total orders, customers, countries, quantity sold, average sales quantity, and sales per order. 
2.	Analysed yearly, quarterly, and monthly sales trends. 
3.	Examined sales performance across territories and countries to assess the impact of market accessibility. 
4.	Identified top-performing product categories to understand customer preferences. 
5.	Analysed the top 10 customers and performed customer segmentation to support loyalty programs and personalized marketing initiatives.
    
### Key Insights
---
1.	Sales grew by 34% in 2004. In 2005, sales increased by 26% compared to the same five-month period in 2004. Sales typically declined from Q1 to Q3 before peaking sharply in Q4, driven by strong performance in October and November. 
2.	The EMEA region generated the highest sales (50%), followed by North America (38%), while APAC contributed the least (12%). The top-performing countries were the USA, Spain, and France, each generating over $1 million in sales. 
3.	Classic Cars (11,663 units) and Vintage Cars (10,777 units) were the most purchased product categories, indicating strong customer preference for road vehicles. Planes (47 units) and Trains (34 units) recorded the lowest sales out of 99,067 total units sold. 
4.	The most valuable customers were Euro Shopping Channel (26 orders worth $912,294) and Mini Gifts Distributors Ltd. (17 orders worth $654,858). Customer segmentation revealed 22 loyal customers, 66 returning customers, and 1 new customer, indicating strong customer retention.
   
### Recommendations
---
1.	Marketing campaigns, promotions, and new product launches should be concentrated in Q4, particularly in October and November to capitalize on seasonal demand. 
2.	Advertising strategies should be strengthened in the APAC region to improve market penetration and sales performance. enhance the company's brand relevance. 
3.	Production and availability of classic and vintage cars should be increased to support customer retention and reinforce brand reliability. 
4.	Offer discounts and promotional incentives for high-demand product categories such as Classic Cars and Vintage Cars. 
5.	Strengthen loyalty programs to convert returning customers into loyal customers and attract new buyers so as to enhance the company's brand relevance.
